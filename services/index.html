How to choose the right service

Water damage jobs overlap, so the names can be confusing. The quickest way to sort them is by what is happening right now. Water still spreading or standing on the floor points to emergency water removal. Water stopped but the structure is wet points to water mitigation, which is the drying and moisture-control stage.

Wet debris, soaked carpet pad and odor after the water is gone are handled in water damage cleanup. Once the structure is dry, water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and finishes. Storm water, groundwater and sewage backup fall under flood damage restoration, and visible growth after a leak is a mold remediation job.

Many homes need more than one. A burst pipe, for example, usually runs through extraction, drying, cleanup and repair in that order, and the dispatch line can explain the sequence before work starts.

What every job includes

Each job begins with a safety check and a moisture assessment. We explain the scope and how the price is set before any work starts, and we keep moisture readings and photos so your insurance file is complete. Crews are licensed and insured restoration professionals, and the industry standards for the work are ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold.

If your situation is not on this list, call anyway. We would rather tell you honestly that a problem belongs to a plumber, roofer or waterproofing contractor than start work that will not solve it.

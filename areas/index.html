Home base: The Colony, TX

The Colony is a home rule city in Denton County on the shore of Lewisville Lake. City Hall is at 6053 Main Street, and homeowners can reach Building Inspections at 972-624-3100 about repair permits and Public Works at 972-624-3129 for storm water and utility questions. If a water main or sewer problem starts on the city side of your property, the city water and sewer line is 972-625-2741 during business hours and 972-624-4433 after hours.

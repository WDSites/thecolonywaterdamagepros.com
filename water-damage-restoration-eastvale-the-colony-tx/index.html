A neighborhood with layers

What that means for a homeowner is simple. A house on the east side can be from many different eras, and neighborhood guides now describe newer subdivisions there. We did not find a verified count of early lake homes, so we do not assume your house is old or new.

Start with the age of your own plumbing

Water damage tracks the condition of pipes, valves and appliances, not a neighborhood label. When you call, tell us roughly when the house was built, whether the water heater and supply lines have been replaced, and where the main shutoff is.

Those answers help us decide whether we are looking at a failed connector, a corroded line, an old water heater tank or a roof problem. Do not assume that older homes always leak more. Newer homes have their own failure points.

What freezes and storms do here

The National Weather Service 1991 to 2020 normals for the Dallas Fort Worth area show about 37 inches of precipitation a year and an average of 29 freezes a season. In February 2021 FEMA declared a major disaster for Texas winter storms, and Denton County was designated for assistance.

A hard freeze can split a supply line in an attic, garage or exterior wall. The break often shows itself when the line thaws and water starts to flow. If that happens, shut the main valve and call.

Safety in the first hour

Water, electricity and the lake

If water has pooled around outlets or a panel, stay back and call the electric utility or 911 before anything else.

Know where the main shutoff is before you need it. If you can reach it safely, close it, and switch off power to wet rooms only from a dry spot.

Move valuables up and out of reach of the water, keep pets and children away from dirty water, and take photos from a safe spot.

How the drying works

Restoration starts with finding the source and classifying the water. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard that defines the water categories and drying classes.

Extraction removes standing water. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure while moisture meters track the readings, and drying stops only when materials return to their dry standard. Wet insulation and drywall are often replaced and some flooring can be saved.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming the age of the house tells you what failed.
  • Skipping the water heater and supply line check after a leak.
  • Painting over a stain without moisture readings.

From 3,646 residents to 46,453: what rapid growth means for your house

Little Elm had 3,646 residents in 2000, 25,898 in 2010 and 46,453 in 2020. A town that grows that fast is mostly built from recent homes, so many Little Elm houses are under 25 years old. We do not have a year-built breakdown for the town, so treat that as a general pattern rather than a rule for your street.

Newer homes leak in particular places. Expect failed supply line connections, water heaters that fail early, dishwasher and ice maker lines, roof flashing and attic air handler drains. Because the plumbing is modern, the damage is usually a sudden release rather than slow corrosion, which matters for insurance. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, but gradual leaks often are not, so check your policy.

Lakefront and shoreline homes on Lewisville Lake

Little Elm lies generally along the north and east shore of Lewisville Lake, and the town's Lakefront district is its original downtown, with restaurants, entertainment and apartments. Water near a lake finds low spots, so a heavy rain can leave water against a foundation or in a garage even when a pipe has not failed.

That distinction decides who pays. Rising water or surface flooding is usually excluded from a standard homeowners policy and needs separate flood coverage, while a broken pipe is often covered. Look up your address on the FEMA flood map, and ask the town about the flood insurance rate map. If rain water has entered the house, our flood damage restoration crews treat it as contaminated.

Town permits, water supply and who to call

The Building Safety Division at 100 W. Eldorado Pkwy handles permits for the town. Texas cities commonly require permits for plumbing, electrical or structural work, so ask Building Safety what your repair needs. A permit is separate from drying, which can start right away.

Little Elm is a customer city of the North Texas Municipal Water District and provides its own retail service. If water is surfacing in the yard or at the meter, that is a utility matter. Water inside the walls is a restoration job, and water damage repair follows once the structure is dry.

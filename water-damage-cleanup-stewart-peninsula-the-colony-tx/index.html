Water Damage Cleanup in Stewart Peninsula, The Colony, TX

Stewart Peninsula homes sit along a lake-and-golf setting that is older than the newest parts of The Colony. Water damage here starts with plumbing and drains as often as weather.

Stewart Peninsula at a glance

Setting

Homes around the Stewart Peninsula Golf Course, which the city describes as a 9-hole course on a peninsula bordering Lewisville Lake.

Nearby city parks

Scout Park on North Colony Boulevard and Stewart Creek Park, which the city lists as a lake access park.

Associations

Directory listings show a Stewart Peninsula Homeowners Association and a separate Southshore association. Confirm which one covers your street.

Water damage sources we see in lakeside streets

Supply line and fixture leaks

A failed connector under a sink or behind a toilet can run for hours before the stain appears. Shut the valve for that fixture if you can reach it safely.

Drain and sewer backups

Water that comes up through a floor drain or a tub is contaminated by definition. Do not wade in, and do not try to clear it with a household vacuum.

Roof and storm openings

Heavy rain can find a loose flashing or a missing shingle. Look for ceiling stains after a storm even when nothing dripped.

Freeze events

The National Weather Service normals show an average of 29 freezes a season at the Dallas Fort Worth station, so a split exposed line is always possible.

Safety first, especially with contaminated water

Before you touch anything

Treat any wet outlet or panel as live. Call the electric utility or 911, and keep your hands off breakers until a professional says the area is safe.

If the water came from a toilet, drain or sewer line, keep people and pets away. Ordinary fans, mops and vacuums are not enough for contaminated water.

Shut off the main water valve only from a dry, safe spot. For a sewer problem in the street, contact the city's Public Works and Utilities department.

How cleanup and drying work

We start by finding the source and sorting the water into one of three categories. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for that sorting and for the drying classes that follow.

Pumps and extraction units remove standing water, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry what is left while meters and a thermal camera track progress. Contaminated materials are removed and disposed of, and the area is cleaned before rebuild begins.

Porous materials like carpet pad and insulation are the first to go when contamination is involved. Hard surfaces can often be cleaned and dried in place.

Insurance questions on a lake edge

The Texas Department of Insurance says standard policies cover sudden and accidental water damage but generally not flood or gradual seepage. Federal flood policy claims have a 60 day filing window.

Because Stewart Peninsula borders Lewisville Lake, the cause matters. A burst pipe and a rising lake are different claims under different policies. Photograph before repairs, keep damaged items, and ask your insurer to confirm which policy applies.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Treating sewage as ordinary water and cleaning it up with household tools.
  • Leaving wet insulation or carpet pad in place because the surface looks dry.
  • Assuming the lake explains every stain. Most water damage starts inside the house.
